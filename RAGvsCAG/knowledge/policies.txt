TechNova Return Policy

Customers can return eligible physical products within 30 days
of purchase.

The product must be in its original condition.

Refunds are processed within 5 business days after the returned
product has been inspected.

Digital products are not eligible for refunds.


TechNova Cancellation Policy

Customers can cancel a subscription at any time.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

Customers will continue to have access to the service until the
current billing period ends.

TechNova does not provide partial refunds for unused subscription time.


TechNova Enterprise Support Policy

Enterprise customers receive priority customer support.

Enterprise support is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Enterprise customers can also request a dedicated technical
support contact.
