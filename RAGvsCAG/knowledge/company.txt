TechNova is a fictional technology company that provides software and
digital products for small and medium-sized businesses.

The company was founded in 2020.

TechNova's main office is located in Austin, Texas.

The company focuses on productivity software, business automation,
and AI-powered tools.

TechNova customer support is available Monday through Friday,
from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Central Time.

Customers can contact support through email or the customer
support portal.
