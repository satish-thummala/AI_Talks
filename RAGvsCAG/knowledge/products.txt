TechNova Products

TechNova offers three main products.

TechNova Flow is a workflow automation platform that helps businesses
automate repetitive tasks and connect different applications.

TechNova Assist is an AI-powered business assistant that can answer
questions, summarize documents, and help employees find information.

TechNova Analytics is a business analytics platform that allows
companies to create dashboards and analyze business data.

TechNova Flow is available in Basic, Professional, and Enterprise
plans.

TechNova Assist is available in Professional and Enterprise plans.

TechNova Analytics is available in Basic and Enterprise plans.
